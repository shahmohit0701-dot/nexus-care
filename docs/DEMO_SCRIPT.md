# 7-minute viva demo

1. Login as Patient.
2. Open Guardian Vision and grant camera access.
3. Enter: “I have severe chest pain for 20 minutes and difficulty breathing.”
4. Show extracted entities, ranked possible conditions, risk and ESI.
5. Explain why this is a triage recommendation rather than a diagnosis.
6. Open a second browser tab and login as Clinician.
7. Show the live queue update / red-alert feed.
8. Press the patient's Emergency button and allow location.
9. Show ambulance simulation and hospital alert.
10. Open FHIR bundle.
11. Login as Admin and show model metrics and platform KPIs.
12. Explain that the ML model was trained on synthetic educational disease/symptom examples and is not clinically validated.
