NEXUS CARE — CLEAN START

1. Extract the ZIP to a local folder (for example C:\NEXUS-CARE). Avoid running directly from a compressed archive.
2. If Windows Smart App Control blocks the .bat file, open PowerShell in this folder and run:
   Get-ChildItem -Recurse -File | Unblock-File
3. Double-click start_demo_8020.bat.
4. Open http://127.0.0.1:8020 if it does not open automatically.

NEW PATIENT
Click “Register as a new patient” on the login screen. Registration requires essential identity, body, medical-history and emergency details. The new patient is automatically signed in after registration.

PROFILE EDITING
Patients can use “My Profile” in the top navigation to edit identity, medical history, body profile and emergency information. Changes persist in the local database.

GUARDIAN VISION
Guardian Vision attempts to start automatically after patient login. Browser security still controls camera permission; a website cannot bypass Chrome/Edge camera permission controls. No separate NEXUS CARE permission form is shown.

SAFETY
Academic prototype only. AI outputs are educational triage recommendations and possible-condition predictions, not medical diagnoses. Human clinical review is required.
